## Why this line

stops. The joke grows out of that fact — one look, total system halt — instead
of taking a human pickup line ("my heart races") and pasting tech jargon on
it. That is exactly what the judge brief rewards first after "is it a robot's
pickup line": the joke must grow out of being a machine. The line is also
short (73 of 200 characters), single-turn, and charming rather than creepy.
